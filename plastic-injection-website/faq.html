FAQ

Common Questions, Clear Answers

Still have questions? Contact our team — we respond within 24 hours.

To provide an accurate quote, please send us your 3D files (STP, STEP, IGES, X_T) and/or 2D drawings (PDF, DWG), including material preference, quantity, and surface finish requirements. If you only have samples, we can quote based on samples too.
Our MOQ is flexible depending on the product. Generally, MOQ starts from 500-1,000 pieces for standard parts. For custom projects or new product development, we can discuss lower trial quantities.
Typical mold development takes 20-30 days for standard molds and up to 45 days for complex molds. Prototyping can be completed in 3-7 days using rapid tooling or 3D printing.
Production lead time is usually 15-30 days after sample approval, depending on the order quantity and product complexity. We always confirm the exact delivery date in your quotation.
We work with a wide range of materials including ABS, PC, PC/ABS, PP, PE, PA (Nylon), POM (Acetal), PBT, PET, TPU, TPE and more. Our engineers will recommend the best material for your application.
Yes. We offer ODM services including industrial design, 3D modeling, and DFM (Design for Manufacturing) analysis. We help optimize your design to reduce cost and improve quality.
Yes, absolutely. We provide samples for your approval before starting mass production. Sample costs are usually refunded or deducted from the first production order.
Our quality control covers incoming materials (IQC), in-process inspection (IPQC), final quality control (FQC) and outgoing quality control (OQC) with full dimensional and visual checks.
Our standard payment terms are 50% deposit and 50% before shipment. We accept T/T (bank transfer), Western Union and other agreed methods. Long-term partnerships can enjoy flexible terms.
We offer flexible shipping options including EXW, FOB, CIF and DDP. We arrange export documentation, customs clearance and reliable worldwide shipping. Delivery is typically 5-15 days by sea or 3-7 days by air.
Yes. We use flame-retardant grades of materials such as FR-PC and FR-ABS for applications requiring fire safety compliance. Please specify the required standard (e.g., UL94 V0).
Yes, we offer complete secondary services including painting, plating, silk-screening, laser engraving, texture, assembly, labeling and packaging — all in-house for full quality control.
