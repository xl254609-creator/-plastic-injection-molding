Quality Policy

Zero Defects. Every Part. Every Time.

Quality is not a department at Haiqiang — it's a company-wide commitment. Our rigorous quality management system ensures every part we produce meets your exact specifications.

From incoming material inspection to final outgoing quality control, we implement checks at every stage of the production process to prevent defects before they happen.

QC Process

Inspection at Every Stage

A comprehensive quality control process from start to finish.

1

IQC

Incoming material inspection for raw resin and purchased components.

2

IPQC

In-process inspection during molding to catch issues early.

3

FQC

Final quality control with full dimensional and visual inspection.

4

OQC

Outgoing quality control before packaging and shipment.
