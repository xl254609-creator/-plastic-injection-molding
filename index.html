What We Make

Two Product Categories We Know Best

We focus on what we do best: small plastic household items and plastic toys — pure plastic or 80%+ plastic content — plus silicone products and silicone overmolding.

Small Plastic Household Items

Storage boxes, containers and everyday household plastic goods. Pure plastic or 80%+ plastic content, molded in ABS, PP/PE, PC or acrylic.

Plastic Toys

Toy parts and complete plastic toys. Pure plastic or 80%+ plastic content, molded in ABS, PP/PE, PC or acrylic.

Capabilities

Mold and Parts From One Factory

Our in-house mold room and 6 injection molding machines cover your project from mold to mass production.

In-House Mold Making

Our own mold room designs and manufactures the molds for your products — mold and parts from the same factory, including rapid molds for small-batch production.

Injection Molding

6 injection molding machines (60–200T) with a monthly capacity of 100,000+ pieces. Silicone overmolding and direct small-batch production available.

Materials we mold: ABS, PP/PE, PC, acrylic — plus silicone products and silicone overmolding.
