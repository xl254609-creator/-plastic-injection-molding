Buyer Guide

What to Send for an Injection Molding Quotation

To quote your project accurately, we need three things from you.

📐

1. Your drawing, sample, or idea

2D or 3D drawings give the most accurate quotation. A physical sample works too. Even a clear description of your idea is a good start.

🧪

2. The material

We mold ABS, PP/PE, PC and acrylic. Tell us which plastic you want — or describe what the part will be used for.

📦

3. The quantity

Our standard MOQ is 1,000 sets. Small trial orders are also welcome at a higher unit price.

📝

What happens next

We review what you sent and send our quotation. After your approval, our in-house mold room designs and builds the mold, we mold trial samples for your approval, and then start mass production. Timeline confirmed per project after drawing review.

Ready? Send us your project

Send your drawing, sample, or idea — plus material and quantity — and we will get back to you with a quotation.
