ABS vs PP vs PC for Household Items

A plain-language comparison of the plastics we mold every day: ABS, PP/PE, PC and acrylic.

🧱

ABS — rigid and clean-looking

ABS is hard and rigid with a smooth surface finish. It is commonly used for structural parts and housings of household products.

📦

PP / PE — flexible and practical

PP and PE are flexible and resist many chemicals. They are commonly used for containers, lids and caps.

🔍

PC — transparent and tough

PC is transparent and impact-resistant. It is commonly used for parts that need clarity and toughness.

✨

Acrylic — clear and glossy

Acrylic is clear with a glossy appearance. It is commonly used for display parts and decorative pieces.

❓

Which one is right for your product?

It depends on what your product is for, how it should look, and how many you need. Tell us your application and the quantity — we will include the material in our quotation. Our standard MOQ is 1,000 sets; small trial orders are also welcome at a higher unit price.
