Our Story

Building on 20+ Years of Manufacturing Excellence

Founded in 2005 in Dongguan, Guangdong — the heart of China's manufacturing industry — Haiqiang Technology has grown from a small mold shop into a full-service plastic injection molding manufacturer.

We combine advanced engineering, precision mold making and high-volume injection molding to serve clients across the globe. Our team of experienced engineers works closely with you from product design through to mass production and delivery.

Our mission is simple: to help our clients bring high-quality plastic products to market faster, at lower cost, and with absolute reliability.

  • ISO 14001 Certified
  • 20+ Years of Manufacturing Experience
  • 10+ Countries Served
  • 1500+ Molds Delivered
  • Complete In-House Facility
  • Dedicated Engineering Team
